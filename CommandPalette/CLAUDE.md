# CommandPalette

How to build and test is in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

`CommandPalette`: a search box over a list of the app's commands (`CCommandPaletteCtrl`, window class `WC_CommandPalette`), `CMDP`-prefixed C-style API (`CMDPITEM`, `CMDPPROVIDER`, `CMDPS_*`, `CMDPN_*`; not `CPL`, Control Panel's; internal names are `Cmp*`, since ColorPicker owns `Cp*`). Commands are added one by one or read from a menu (`AddFromMenu`: names without `&`, the text after a tab as the shortcut, the submenu path as the category, grayed and checked states); `RefreshMenus(window)` reads them again, first sending the window `WM_INITMENUPOPUP` for each submenu as a menu opening would, and keeps each menu's commands in their place. Typing filters by fuzzy matching (`src/CommandPaletteModel.*`, `CmpPattern`): the typed words in any order, each a subsequence of the label ("Category: Name"), scored by a DP that rewards word starts (camel humps and digits too), runs and a capital typed on a capital, and charges gaps; the matched letters are drawn bold in the accent color. Ties go to the recently used (`CmpRecent`, saved as "12;7;301"), which also come first with nothing typed. The box is a real `Edit` subclassed with `CContainedWindowT` (`CContainedWindow::Create` makes an `ATL:Edit` superclass, which the system's edit accessibility and `FindWindowEx("Edit")` don't know); the list is a child window (`CmpListWindow`, `src/CommandPaletteList.h`) with its own scroll bar, handled in `ALT_MSG_MAP(2)`, and the focus stays in the box. Enter or a click sends `CMDPN_EXECUTE` (non-zero: the app ran it), then posts `WM_COMMAND` to the owner. `CCommandPalettePopup` hosts a palette at the top of the owner's client area, sized to its results (`CMDPN_RESULTSCHANGED`), rounded with DWM's border on Windows 11, keeping the owner's caption active (`WM_NCACTIVATE`), and closes on Escape, a pick or deactivation (`CMDPN_CLOSED`), forwarding the palette's notifications to the owner. Prefix providers (`AddProvider`: '@', ':' ...; '?' lists them) supply items the control asks for as needed and keeps, through `CMDPN_GETITEM` or a `pfnGetItem` callback (much faster for long lists: a notification per item pays for every hook on the thread, WTLHelper's dark mode included, and for the popup's forwarding); the text after the prefix goes out with `CMDPN_QUERY`, and `CMDPPF_NOFILTER` providers answer it with their own items. A long list is matched in 25 ms slices on a timer (`CmpSearch`: each step's new results are sorted and merged in, not the whole list again), with a progress line under the box. `Filter` empties the old results before switching lists: they index the old one. High contrast uses the system colors over the app's (`ForceHighContrast` for testing). MSAA (`CommandPaletteAccessible.cpp`) is a list of the result rows on the list window; the selected row is reported focused while the box has the focus (an active descendant), with focus and selection events as it moves, and the box and the list are named through `IAccPropServices` (which needs COM on the thread). The model is covered by `CommandPaletteTests`; `CommandPaletteDemo` (which links WTLHelper on x64) is for checking by hand.
