# DiffView

How to build and test is in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

`DiffView`: compares two texts line by line (`CDiffViewCtrl`, window class `WC_DiffView`), `DFV`-prefixed C-style API (`DFVPOS`, `DFVCHANGE`, `DFVCOLORS`, `DFVN_*` notifications), side by side or unified (`DFVL_*`), GDI-drawn. The app gives it the two texts and the control diffs them itself (`src/DiffViewModel.*`): Myers' algorithm in linear space (as GNU diff's `diffseq`) between anchors, the lines found once on each side kept in order by a longest increasing subsequence, with a cost limit that splits a range at the furthest point either search reached so unrelated texts stay fast (so a diff can be a little longer than the shortest). Pure inserted or deleted runs are slid to end on a blank line (git's sliding). Ignore case and whitespace (`DFVF_*`) compare normalized lines; a last line with no line break differs from the same line with one. The lines of a change are paired by similarity (a DP, or by position past 64x64), and each pair gets its changed words, or characters when a word mostly stayed. `DfBuildRows` lays out the rows: side by side the unpaired lines of a change share rows and the shorter side is padded (hatched filler), unified a change's left lines then its right ones; runs of unchanged lines fold to `SetContextLines` lines of context, and a fold opens on a click or Enter (`DFVN_FOLDEXPANDED`). `DfDocument` holds the texts, the diff, the rows and a line-to-row map per side. Positions (`DFVPOS`) are a side, a line and a column, so the caret and selection (`DiffViewSelection.cpp`) survive a new diff, folding and switching layouts; side by side a selection stays in its pane, unified it runs over rows. F7/Shift+F7 go between changes, F6 to the other pane. An overview strip beside the scroll bar shows every change and the part in view. `EnableMerge` (`DiffViewMerge.cpp`) puts arrows in the strip between the panes; the user's merge (an arrow, Alt+Right or Alt+Left) sends `DFVN_MERGE` (non-zero stops it) then `DFVN_TEXTCHANGED`; merges are undone with `Undo`/Ctrl+Z, each keeping only the lines it replaced. Texts past `SetBackgroundThreshold` (100,000 lines) are compared on a detached worker thread (`DfJob`, cancelled through `DfOptions::cancel`) that posts `WM_DFV_DIFFDONE`; meanwhile the last diff stays shown with a note and the text queries answer about the new texts (`Latest`), and `DFVN_DIFFDONE` follows. Like LogView, it follows WTLHelper's dark mode through `GetSysColor` and the registered `ThemeChanged` message; high contrast uses the system colors even over the app's, framing the changed words instead of shading them (`ForceHighContrast` for testing). MSAA (`DiffViewAccessible.cpp`) is a list of the rows on the control itself, named from the standard proxy (never the window's own `OBJID_WINDOW`, which would recurse); a fold's default action opens it. The model is covered by `DiffViewTests`; `DiffViewDemo` (which links WTLHelper on x64) is for checking by hand.

Syntax highlighting (`DiffViewStyle.cpp`, the pure parts in the model) is the app's: the control has no tokenizer. `SetStylePalette` (`DFVSTYLE`: text color, background, bold/italic/underline) turns it on, and the control sends `DFVN_GETLINESTYLE` (`NMDFVLINESTYLE`) at paint time for a line it has no spans for, the app answering with `DFVSTYLESPAN`s (character indexes into the line, a palette index each). `DfNormalizeStyleSpans` sorts them and cuts them to the line (on an overlap the span that starts first keeps the characters), `DfStyleSegments` turns them into column ranges of the tab-expanded line with plain segments between, and `PaintText` draws each segment in its color and its own font (`StyleFont`: the control's font made bold, italic or underlined, kept in `m_StyleFonts` and dropped in `UpdateFonts`); a style's background is under the changed words' shading, and the selection overdraws the same segments in the selection's color so a bold glyph doesn't ghost. The spans are kept per side and line in `DfStyleStore` (emptied past 20,000 lines) until the texts change (`ForgetStyles`, in `Compare` and `OnDiffDone`) or `InvalidateStyles`; a new palette keeps them, since they hold indexes, which is how a theme change recolors without tokenizing again. High contrast ignores the palette. While a compare runs on the worker thread the lines asked about are the previous texts' (`NMDFVLINESTYLE` carries the line's text for that). The demo (View > Syntax Highlighting) has a small stateless C++ tokenizer; painting with the fonts, dark mode and unified layout was checked by screenshots, selection over styled text not yet.

Annotations (`DiffViewAnnotations.cpp`, the rows in the model) are room under a line for a window of the app's, such as a review comment. The row height is fixed, so an annotation is as many `DfRowKind::Annotation` rows as its height needs (`DfAnnotationSlot` in, `AnnotationRows`: the height in DIPs rounded up to whole rows), which leaves scrolling, paging, the overview and hit testing in whole rows; `DfBuildRows` puts them right under the row showing the line (the left side's first, then in the order given), side by side in the side's pane (the other hatched) or across both (`DFVAF_FULLWIDTH`), and keeps an annotated unchanged line, with `min(context, 2)` lines around it, out of the folds. Annotation rows have no line (`LineOfRow` false, so stepping, selection and copy skip them), count in the rows of the change they follow (`changeRows`), and `GetAnchor` anchors at the line above when the top row is one. The windows are the app's and made on demand: `UpdateAnnotations` (run from `UpdateScrollBars`, so after every scroll, resize and rebuild) sends `DFVN_ANNOTATIONSHOW` for one within a page of the view, the app makes its window a child of the control and returns it, and `DFVN_ANNOTATIONHIDE` when it goes farther (so a file with hundreds of comments has a few windows); `PlaceAnnotationWindows` moves them with one `DeferWindowPos` and cuts the part under the titles or past the rows with a window region. The app's first height is an estimate: it measures in the SHOW notification and calls `SetAnnotationHeight`, which rebuilds the rows (keeping the line at the top) and is safe inside the notification (`m_UpdatingAnnotations` makes the nested update a request to go round again). The control has `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` and passes `WM_COMMAND`, `WM_NOTIFY`, `WM_DRAWITEM`, `WM_MEASUREITEM` and `WM_CTLCOLOR*` from the windows on to its parent. The lines are those of the text the view shows, so after a merge, an undo or new texts the app gives the annotations again; one whose line isn't there has no rows. Checked by hand with the demo (View > Sample Comments): both layouts, dark mode, the 200,000-line sample (no windows far from the comments, four back at the top), a window cut at the titles; the accessible names of the rows and selecting text across annotation rows weren't tried.

The gutter's extras (`DiffViewGutter.cpp`): `EnableGutterAdd` shows a "+" over the line number of the line under the mouse (`UpdateHotGutter` from `OnMouseMove`, cleared by `WM_MOUSELEAVE` through the same `m_TrackingMouse` as the merge arrows; the box is `GutterAddRect`, right-aligned where the number ends, and only a click on the box counts, `GutterAddAt`), and a click sends `DFVN_GUTTERADD` with the side and the first and last line, which `DfCommentRange` (the model) takes from the selection when the click was inside it, on the line's side (a selection ending at the start of a line doesn't include it), else the line alone; an unchanged unified line is the right side's, as GitHub's comments on context lines. `SetGutterIcon` marks lines (the app's `HICON`, kept alive by the app, scaled to the row); `GutterExtra` makes the gutter one row wider while any is set, the icon at its right edge, and with line numbers hidden the "+" widens it too. Checking the hover by script needs the real cursor over the window (`SetCursorPos`): a synthetic `WM_MOUSEMOVE` is undone at once by the `WM_MOUSELEAVE` that `TrackMouseEvent` posts when the cursor isn't there. Demo: View > Comment Buttons (+), and sample comments get icons; a click adds a comment under the last line. By hand: side by side, a click, a click inside a selection; unified and high contrast weren't tried. The keyboard: `StartComment` does what a click on the "+" does for the caret's line (or the selection's), and with `EnableGutterAdd` on Ctrl+Shift+M calls it (`OnKeyDown`); the demo's Edit > Add Comment (an accelerator for the same key, which reaches the app first) was checked, the control's own key handling was not.

Patches (`SetPatch`; `DfParsePatch`, `DfDiffFromBlocks` and `DfPatchData` in the model) show a unified diff of one file, hunks only, as a service gives a file's changes. The parser skips the headers before the hunks, reads each hunk by the counts in its header (so a line that looks like a header inside one is a line), handles "\ No newline at end of file", CRLF, counts of 0 (the number is then the line before) and a trimmed empty context line, stops at the next "diff " line, and checks that the hunks come in order and leave the same number of lines between them on both sides. The texts it gives have the patch's lines at the line numbers of the hunks' headers and empty lines in between (`DfPatchData::unknown`, kept as ranges); the diff is not computed but made from the patch's blocks (`DfDocument::patch`; `Compute` uses `DfDiffFromBlocks`, which does only the pairing and the changes within lines), so it is the one git made and nothing can slide into the unknown lines. `DfBuildRows` takes the unknown ranges: they are never shown, and each run in an equal block is a `Fold` row with `gap` set (drawn "N lines not in the patch"), whatever the context or expanding; the known lines around it fold as usual. `ExpandFold` on a gap (a click, Enter, the accessible action) sends `DFVN_PATCHGAP` (both sides' first line and the count) instead of opening it, for an app that fetches the file and calls `SetTexts`, which leaves patch mode (`Compare` drops the patch unless `SetOptions` is the caller, which keeps it and never goes to the worker thread). In patch mode: merging is off (`MergeChange`, the arrows and `UserMerge` check `patch`), `SetText` for one side does nothing (the other side has blanks), `GetUnifiedDiff` returns the patch text as given, the ignore options only change the words marked within lines, and the lines after the last hunk aren't shown (their number isn't in the patch). Annotations and the other line-number features work with the files' numbers; an annotation on an unknown line has no rows. Checked by hand: the demo's File > Sample Patch (the sample as `GetUnifiedDiff(2)`, read back with `SetPatch`) with comments, and a click on a gap reaching the notification; opening a patch file from the dialog wasn't tried, nor a real GitHub patch (a truncated or very large one).
