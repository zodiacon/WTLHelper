# GraphControl

How to build and test is in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

`GraphControl`: time-series graph (Direct2D/DirectWrite, namespace `GraphCtrl`). `GraphData` is a separate pure model, which is what the tests target. It also has the cell graphs (`include/CellGraphs.h`, no window): line and column sparklines, meters and stacked bars drawn in list view and TreeListView cells. `DrawSparkline`/`DrawColumns`/`DrawMeter`/`DrawStacked` draw in software over a cell's pixels (copied into a per-thread DIB section and back). `CellHistory<Key>` keeps a `Series` per row and drops the rows a refresh didn't see. The `CellGraphs` helper gives columns a kind and a style, and draws them from the parent's `NM_CUSTOMDRAW`, with the cells' data from a callback. It copies a cell before the list paints it (sub-item pre-paint) and after (sub-item post-paint): the list keeps painting its own selection, hot and background colors, and `Cells::CellBackground` takes the text out before the graph goes on. That works both when a themed list (Explorer, dark mode) draws the row's background first and when a list fills each cell itself (the classic highlight). TreeListView relays the sub-item post-paint stage for this. Colors left at `CLR_DEFAULT` follow the cell's background. A meter can carry text over its bar (`CellData::Text`, drawn with GDI into the same DIB in the list's font, in two colors split where the bar ends). Sparklines and columns can have a dashed reference line, and a sparkline's color can follow thresholds by its newest sample. `EnableTooltips` subclasses the list view (a TreeListView's inner list, with a row-to-item mapping) and moves one tooltip tool to the graph cell under the mouse: the last, lowest, highest and average samples shown (`Cells::Stats`), in the column's `SetFormat` format, or the data's own `Tooltip`. To keep painting cheap, the helper doesn't copy the cell out and back a second time to take the text out: a `Cells::BackgroundScope` hands the before-copy to the renderer, which does it in its own buffer. The probe pixel is taken from the part of the cell the DC can paint (a row cut off at the bottom of the list). Pixels are blended in fixed point, and meter text goes through `ExtTextOutW` (`DrawTextW` only to add an ellipsis); the demo's View > Measure List Painting times a repaint (View > Graphs turns them off to compare). Colors too close to the cell's background are moved apart (`Visible`), and in high contrast every graph uses the system text color that reads on the cell (`Farthest`; `ForceHighContrast` lets the tests choose). `CGraphGrid` places its tiles with `LayoutGridTiles` (`include/GraphGridLayout.h`, window-free): `SetTileGroups` makes runs of tiles with the same key groups (a core's logical processors), drawn closer together and kept in one row. The window-free parts (`src/CellGraphsModel.*`: scaling, geometry, the rasterizer, `CellBackground`; the grid's layout) are covered by `GraphControlTests`; `CellGraphsDemo` is for checking by hand.
