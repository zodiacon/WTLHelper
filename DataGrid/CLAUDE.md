# DataGrid

How to build and test is in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

`DataGrid`: a grid of typed columns (`CDataGridCtrl`, window class `WC_DataGrid`), GDI-drawn, `DGV`-prefixed C-style API (`DGVCOLUMN`, `DGVVALUE`, `DGVHITTEST`, `DGVCOLORS`, `DGVN_*` notifications from `DGVN_FIRST` = `0U - 3500U`). Columns are known by IDs the control gives them (`InsertColumn`, from 1, never reused) and kept in `DgColumnSet` with a display order of all of them, hidden ones too; each has a slot, also never reused, where the store keeps its values, so deleting a column only clears its slot (`DgStore::ClearSlot`). Rows are either the store's (`DgStore`, a vector of cells per row, the default) or the app's in virtual mode (`SetVirtual`, `SetRowCount`), which the control asks for with `DGVN_GETDISPINFO` as it paints (`Fetch`) and keeps in `DgCellCache`, trimmed after every paint to the rows around the view and dropped by `RedrawRows`. The view works in rows in view, mapped to rows in the data by `DgRowMap` (the identity for now: sorting and filtering will set an order), while the API, the caret and the notifications use data rows, so they survive sorting.

The window-free model (`src/DataGridModel.*`) mirrors the public constants in its own namespaced enums (`DgType`, `DgColType`, `DgAlign`, `DgColFlag`, `DgHitFlag`) so the tests needn't include ATL; `DataGridCtrl.cpp` `static_assert`s that they agree, so a new value goes in both. `DgLayoutColumns` places the visible columns: fixed ones at their width (DIPs, never under their minimum), fill ones (`DGVCOLUMN::fill`) sharing the width the fixed ones leave by cumulative shares so they add up exactly, each at least its minimum; frozen columns (`SetFrozenColumns`) that would leave no room to scroll the rest are dropped from the right. A column's `left` is unscrolled; `DgLayout::Left` adds the row header and subtracts the horizontal scroll for the columns past the frozen ones, which scroll under them (`ScrollLeft`). Rows scroll whole: `topRow` is a 64-bit row, and `DgScrollMap` scales a top row past `MaxPos` (`0x3FFFFFFF`) onto the 32-bit scroll bar with both ends exact, so billions of rows work (the demo's Data menu has five billion). `DgHitTest` gives the header's dividers priority over the titles (the nearest within the zone, a tie to the later column so a narrow one can still be widened), skipping scrolled columns' dividers that are under the frozen ones; `DgDropIndex` finds where a dragged column goes by the columns' middles. `DgNavigate` moves the caret as Excel does (Ctrl+arrows to the edges, Home/End, Ctrl+Home/End); PgUp/PgDn move the view with the caret, as a list view does.

Formatting (`DgFormat`) goes by the column's type, then the value's: an enum's integer names an item of `pszEnumItems`, a progress column shows a percent of an integer 0-100 or a float 0-1, an integer column rounds a float, a float column shows `decimals` digits, `DGVCF_GROUPDIGITS` groups with the user's separator (`DgNumberFormat`, read from the locale on `WM_SETTINGCHANGE`). Dates are FILETIMEs taken as local time and shown in the user's short date and time (`DgCellText`); `DgFormatDateTime` is the model's ISO fallback, for the tests. Check boxes and progress bars are drawn, not themed parts, so they follow the colors (and dark mode).

Painting (`DataGridPaint.cpp`) goes through a memory DC: the scrolling columns, then the frozen ones over them (filled first, since the scrolled ones run under them), the row numbers (`DGVS_ROWHEADER`, as wide as the row count's digits), the caret's frame (2px in the highlight focused, 1px gray not), then the header in a semi-bold font, with the dragged title following the mouse and a bar where it would go. Colors are read from `GetSysColor` on every paint, blended from the window's colors (grid lines, the header, the caret's row), so WTLHelper's dark mode works with no dependency; `ThemeChanged` (registered by name), `WM_THEMECHANGED` and `WM_SYSCOLORCHANGE` repaint. `SetColors` overrides any of them (`CLR_DEFAULT`: the system's). High contrast ignores them and uses the system's colors. Sizes are DIPs, scaled with the window's DPI; `WM_DPICHANGED_AFTERPARENT` remakes the fonts and the layout.

The header's mouse (`DataGridHeader.cpp`): a divider drag resizes live (a fill column becomes fixed; `DGVN_COLUMNRESIZED` on release), a double-click fits it to its title and the rows in view (`MeasureColumn`), a title press that moves past `SM_CXDRAG` becomes a column drag (scrolling near the edges; `DGVN_COLUMNMOVED`), otherwise a click (`DGVN_COLUMNCLICK`; non-zero from the app stops the sort it would do). `DGVS_NOCOLUMNRESIZE`/`DGVS_NOCOLUMNREORDER` and the columns' `DGVCF_NORESIZE`/`DGVCF_NOREORDER` stop them. `Relayout` reruns up to three times when a scroll bar showing or hiding resizes the client area (`sizedAgain`).

Cells (`DataGridInput.cpp`): a click selects the cell and moves the caret there (`DGVN_CARETCHANGED`, `DGVN_SELCHANGED`; `SetCaret` sends neither and puts the selection on the caret), a double-click or Enter sends `DGVN_ACTIVATE`, a plain click on a link column's cell `DGVN_LINKCLICK`, a right-click outside the selection selects the cell (inside, it only moves the caret, as in Excel) then `WM_CONTEXTMENU` sends `DGVN_CONTEXTMENU` (from the keyboard: under the caret's cell). Focus without a caret puts it at the top row's first column.

The selection (`DataGridSelection.cpp`, the model's `DgSelection`) is rectangles (`DgRange`) of rows in view and indexes into the visible columns, the last one the one Shift extends from the anchor; the caret is the end that moves (so it follows a drag or Shift+arrows, unlike Excel's active cell). Being in view terms it means nothing once the rows are sorted or the visible columns change: `ApplyOrder` and `Relayout` (when the visible IDs differ from `shownIds`) put it back on the caret's cell, which is kept by data row and ID; fewer rows only clip it (`Clamp`). `DGVSM_CELLS` (the default): a click resets, Shift extends, Ctrl adds a range, a drag extends (with `WM_TIMER` scrolling past the edges, `DgPosFromPoint` clamping the point to the cells in view), the row numbers select whole rows without moving the caret's column (`wholeRows`), the corner and Ctrl+A everything, Shift+Space the caret's row, Ctrl+Space its column. `DGVSM_ROWS`: every range spans the columns, Ctrl+click and Ctrl+Space toggle a row (splitting a range), and the selection may be empty. `DGVSM_SINGLE`: only the caret's cell. A selection of the caret's cell alone isn't shaded (the caret's frame shows it); the rest is a blend of the highlight (`crSelection`), paler without the focus, and their row numbers and (cells mode) titles are tinted. `Copy` (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+Insert; Ctrl+Shift+C with the titles) puts the rows and columns the selection touches, cells outside it empty, on the clipboard as TSV (`DgTsvField` quotes as Excel does) and `HTML Format` (`DgCfHtml` with its byte offsets, UTF-8); past a million cells it beeps and copies nothing.

Sorting: a click on a title goes unsorted, ascending, descending, unsorted (`DgClickSort`), Shift+click adds the column as another key or turns it; `DGVS_NOSORT` or `DGVCF_NOSORT` stop it. `DGVN_SORTCHANGING` (non-zero stops it) and `DGVN_SORTCHANGED` are sent for the user's sorts only (`SetSortKeys` sends nothing). The store's rows are sorted into the row map (`DgSortOrder`: a stable sort of the data rows; numbers and check boxes before dates before text, text in the user's order with digits as numbers (`CompareStringEx`, `SORT_DIGITSASNUMBERS`); empty cells last whichever way). After sorting the caret's row is scrolled into view. Virtual mode only shows the keys (an arrow, and their order when there are several): the app sorts its rows on `DGVN_SORTCHANGED` and calls `RedrawRows`, or stops what it can't do on `DGVN_SORTCHANGING`, as the demo does for all but ID. Inserting or deleting rows sorts again; `SetCell` doesn't (rows don't jump while they're changed), `Resort` does. Sorting runs on the UI thread for now (the plan has it sliced for big stores in phase 4).

Editing (`DataGridEdit.cpp`) is off unless `DGVS_EDITABLE`; `DGVCF_READONLY` locks a column. F2 or a double-click opens an editor with the cell's text (`DgEditText`: numbers ungrouped with every digit that reads back the same, ISO dates), typing a character opens it with that character instead (and then the arrows commit and move, as Excel's Enter mode; F2 in the editor switches to moving in the text). The editor is a child over the cell (`m_Editor`, `ALT_MSG_MAP(1)`): an `Edit` for text, numbers, links and progress (typed: "42%", "42" or "0.5"), a drop-down list for enums, a date picker (`DTS_SHOWNONE` for an empty cell; the user's short date and time format) for dates; check boxes have none: a click on the box, a double-click (a second click) or Space toggles them (Space every selected check box, to the caret's new state), and editable empty ones show an empty box to click. Enter or Tab commit and move (Shift: back), Esc cancels (`DgEditKey`); the focus going elsewhere commits (posted `WM_DGV_ENDEDIT`, after the focus settles), or cancels with a beep if the value can't be kept; a click on the grid with a value that can't be kept does nothing, so the editor stays. Scrolling moves the editor (`PositionEditor` in `ScrollRows`, `ScrollX` and `Relayout`) and closes it, committing, when its cell leaves the view; sorting commits first. An editor nobody changed writes nothing (`dirty`: `EN_CHANGE`, `CBN_SELCHANGE`, `DTN_DATETIMECHANGE`, or typing), so opening and closing one doesn't round a date's fraction of a second or a float's digits. The text is parsed by the column's type (`DgParseValue`: the user's separators, with a no-break space as one; a whole number for integers; enums by name or index; dates as ISO or, through `DgParseUserDate` (`VarDateFromStr`), in the user's format, so what was copied pastes back), then `DGVN_VALIDATE` lets the app change or reject the value; a rejection shows a balloon under the cell (`ShowError`, for five seconds) and keeps the editor. Every change goes through `Apply`: written to the store, or in virtual mode sent to the app as `DGVN_SETCELL` (and the cell's cache dropped), pushed on the undo stack (`DgUndoStack`, a hundred steps, by data row and column ID) and announced with `DGVN_CELLSCHANGED` and its reason. Ctrl+V or Shift+Insert pastes tab-separated text (`DgParseTsv`, Excel's quoting) from the top-left of the selection's last range, or one value into every selected cell (`DgPasteTargets`), skipping read-only cells and values that don't parse or validate (with one beep), as one undo step, and selects what it pasted; Delete clears the selected cells, Shift+Delete or Ctrl+X cuts. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes, selecting the first cell changed. Inserting or deleting rows (or fewer rows, or deleting a column, or switching modes) clears the undo stack, since its rows' indexes would name other rows; so must the app when it re-orders a virtual grid's rows itself (`ClearUndo`, as the demo does when it reverses them). Edits don't re-sort a sorted store. The editors' colors follow the grid's (`WM_CTLCOLOREDIT`, `WM_CTLCOLORLISTBOX`). There's no new-row placeholder yet.

Covered by `DataGridTests` (columns and order, layout with fill, hidden and frozen columns, scrolling and the scroll bar's mapping, hit testing, drop places, navigation, formatting, the store, the cache, the row map, points to cells, the selection in each mode, clamping it, the sort clicks, comparing values, sort orders with several keys, TSV, HTML, UTF-8 and the clipboard's HTML format, the selection as a table, parsing every type's text with the user's separators, ISO and the user's dates, the editors' starting text and its round trip, parsing TSV and its round trip with copying, where pasted fields go, the editor's keys, and undo); `DataGridDemo` (which links WTLHelper on x64) is for checking by hand: store and virtual data over every column type, frozen columns, the styles, custom colors, a large font, dark mode, the selection modes, copying, sorting (virtual: by ID only), deleting the selected rows, editing (ID read-only, Quantity rejecting negative numbers; virtual mode keeping the changed cells in the demo), and a log of the notifications.
